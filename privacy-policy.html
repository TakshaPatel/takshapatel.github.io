Privacy Policy

Que  ·  Effective date: June 17, 2025  ·  Last updated: June 17, 2025

The short version: Que does not collect, store, or share any personal information. All of your progress data lives exclusively on your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

1. Overview

Que is an iOS app that helps students practice SAT grammar and build vocabulary. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the app.

We are committed to your privacy. We do not require an account, we do not collect personal information, and we do not track you.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Que does not collect, transmit, or store any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, or any other personal identifiers
  • Device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV, or similar)
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, or other device content
  • Usage analytics or behavioral tracking data
  • Crash reports sent to our servers

3. Data Stored on Your Device

Que stores the following data locally on your device only, using iOS UserDefaults and an App Group shared container (used exclusively to display your widget):

  • Practice streak – the number of consecutive days you have answered a question
  • Last answered date – the date you last completed a practice question
  • Word of the day – the current vocabulary word and its definition
  • Onboarding status – whether you have completed the first-launch walkthrough

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app.

4. Network Requests

When you open a practice session, Que fetches SAT grammar questions from a third-party public API hosted at pinesat.com. If that service is unavailable, the app falls back to a public content-delivery network. These requests:

  • Contain no personal information or device identifiers
  • Are standard anonymous HTTPS GET requests for question content
  • Are not used to track, profile, or identify you

The pinesat.com service is a third-party provider. Their use of your IP address in serving these requests is governed by their own privacy practices, which are outside our control. Because these are anonymous content requests equivalent to loading a web page, we do not consider this a form of personal data collection.

5. Widgets

Que offers an iOS Home Screen widget that displays your word of the day and practice streak. The widget reads data from the same on-device shared container described in Section 3. No additional data is collected or transmitted for widget functionality.

6. Children's Privacy

Que is designed for students preparing for the SAT and may be used by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the app collects no personal data, it is suitable for users of all ages.

7. Third-Party Services

Que does not integrate any advertising networks, analytics SDKs, social login providers, or other third-party data-collection services.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over it. To delete all data associated with Que, simply uninstall the app from your device. iOS will remove the app's UserDefaults and shared container upon uninstallation.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
